Playing. Power on, start the melody or play a note, and hold Freeze. Recording runs while the button is down (LED solid red; green once layers are playing) and stops the instant you let go, up to 8 s; the layer then swells in over Fade. In Single mode each capture crossfades over the previous loop; in Multi, a seventh capture retires the oldest. Tap Clear to fade out the oldest layer, hold it to clear all. Computer keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play notes, Space works like the Freeze button (hold to record), Backspace clears the oldest, Esc clears all. On iPad, turn off silent mode if you hear nothing.

Firmware map. 8 s stereo ring buffer; grains read with Hermite interpolation; voices above 1:1 read a copy low-passed at 0.2·fs so the octave doesn't alias; −8 gets a 28 Hz high-pass; Texture moves grain size, window shape and density together; spread and drift are fixed; tanh soft clip at the output. Same structure ports to DaisySP almost line for line.
